Support

Need help, want to report a problem or delete your data? Deletion is done in the app itself (menu, Account); for anything else, write to us.

Email supportjoncqs@gmail.comWrite to us

When writing, it helps to know

  • What you tried to do and what happened.
  • Your device and the app version.
  • If you request deletion by email: the email of the account used in the app. Requests by email are handled within 30 days.

Common questions

The AI was slow or failed

Models are free and may be overloaded; try again or switch model.

Voice was not understood

Speak closer to the microphone; audio with no speech is discarded.

I want everything deleted

In the app: menu, Account, "Delete account and data" (as Guest, "Delete my data"). The data is deleted right away. If you prefer, write to the contact above (see privacy).
