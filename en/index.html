What you can do

  • Chat by text or voice (voice is turned into text before it goes to the AI).
  • Pick a model or let AUTO mode choose.
  • Create agents with your own instructions (Agent Studio), with skills, a knowledge base and memory.
  • Search the web for current information, listen to answers aloud and export the conversation (PDF, spreadsheet).
